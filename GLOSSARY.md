# منظفات الإيمان (Manazef al-Iman)

A neighbourhood cleaning-supplies shop in Egypt. Shoppers build a cart on the site and send the order to the shop on WhatsApp; the owner manages the catalog from the admin.

## Language

**Product**:
One sellable item with its own name, price and photo. A different size or scent of the same item is a separate Product.
_Avoid_: Item, SKU, variant

**Variation**:
A Product that shares its name stem, Brand, Category and description with another Product but differs in size, scent or pack.
_Avoid_: Variant, option

**Brand**:
The maker's name a Product is sold under, chosen from the shop's managed list of brands. Written as on the pack (for example Pril), with an optional Arabic name (بريل) so either spelling finds it.
_Avoid_: Company, manufacturer, label

**Category**:
A shelf grouping of Products shown on the storefront.
_Avoid_: Section, department

**Wholesale product**:
A Product in the wholesale Category, priced for a whole carton.
_Avoid_: Bulk item, جملة unit

**Carton content**:
How many retail packs a Wholesale product's carton holds.
_Avoid_: Carton size, pack count

**Search suggestion**:
A Product or Category offered while the shopper or owner is still typing, tolerant of spelling mistakes.
_Avoid_: Autocomplete result, hint

**Shop location**:
The point on the map where the shop is, set by the owner and shown to shoppers on the Visit us map.
_Avoid_: Coordinates, lat/lng
